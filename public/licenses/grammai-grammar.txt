grammai — bundled grammar engines

English: Harper 2.13.0 (Apache-2.0), Hunspell/SCOWL dictionaries and nspell.
Notices and license texts are included alongside this file.

German and Romanian: LingoTweaker 0.2.8 (LGPL-2.1-or-later), an independent Rust/WebAssembly
port of LanguageTool and its rule data. LingoTweaker is not affiliated with or
endorsed by LanguageTool. The packaged engine and the German and Romanian data are unmodified.

Source for the engine, build instructions and data provenance:
https://github.com/fiduswriter/LingoTweaker/tree/819dc354224c9087ff486708adfd413ecb45f466
Source archive:
https://github.com/fiduswriter/LingoTweaker/archive/819dc354224c9087ff486708adfd413ecb45f466.tar.gz

The WebAssembly binary is a separate, replaceable file: german.wasm.
Its JS binding is bundled unmodified into german-worker.js from the
lingotweaker-wasm npm package. The adapter does not change the engine source.
The German rule/dictionary data stays a separate gzip-compressed pack:
german.pack.gz (German) and romanian.pack.gz (Romanian). Their editable XML rules and per-file provenance are inside
that pack. The container format and tools for extraction/rebuilding are in
crates/lt-data/src/pack.rs in the source archive above.

Romanian Hunspell dictionary: GPL-2.0 / LGPL-2.1 / MPL-1.1 tri-license (rospell).
Romanian POS data: LGPL (Ionuț Păduraru).
